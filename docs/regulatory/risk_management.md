This file is structured after ISO 14971 (hazard identification, risk estimation, risk control, residual-risk evaluation). It is an engineering input intended to support a manufacturer-owned risk management process. It does not claim conformity with ISO 14971 or any other standard. No risk management plan has been approved, and no risk acceptability decision has been made by an accountable person.

## 1. Scope

- **Product:** BehaviorLens, code base at `rules-v0.3` (`config.ALGORITHM_VERSION`), pose model `pose_landmarker_lite` pinned by SHA-256 (`config.MODEL_SHA256`), SQLite schema version 2 (`storage.SCHEMA_VERSION`).
- **Function analysed:** a local-first Streamlit application that runs MediaPipe pose estimation on a locally stored video and applies interpretable rules to *suggest* observable movement events (`hand_raised`, `standing_transition`, `sustained_standing`). A human reviewer confirms, rejects, edits or adds events, and exports the reviewed records as CSV or JSON. See [intended_use.md](intended_use.md) and [architecture.md](architecture.md).
- **Current labelled use:** research/demo prototype on synthetic data or staged footage of consenting adults (`config.DISCLAIMER`). The analysis below also considers **reasonably foreseeable misuse**, chiefly use on real clinical recordings and treating suggestions as a complete or validated behavioral record, because that is where most harm would arise.
- **Updated 2026-10-20 for rules-v0.3:** person tracking (`tracking.py`) and camera-motion detection (`camera_motion.py`) are now implemented and analysed under H-03 and H-04. Any future cloud, multi-user or networked deployment needs a new analysis.

## 2. Risk estimation scales (proposed)

The scales and the acceptability matrix are **proposals**. The manufacturer must set them in its risk management plan.

**Severity.** BehaviorLens does not act on a patient directly. Harm happens indirectly, through a wrong behavioral record that informs a decision, through a privacy breach, or through loss of review work.

| Level | Name | Description (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Negligible | Inconvenience or rework, with no effect on any decision or person. |
| S2 | Minor | Wasted reviewer time, or a recoverable loss of review data. A record error that would be caught by normal practice. |
| S3 | Serious | An incorrect behavioral record that could inform an inappropriate (but reversible) programme decision, or limited disclosure of non-identifying data. |
| S4 | Critical | Disclosure of identifiable video of a person (possibly a patient), or a systematically wrong record used for clinical decisions over time. |
| S5 | Catastrophic | Large-scale disclosure of identifiable patient video, or a record error that contributes to serious injury. |

**Probability** of the harm occurring during the expected period of use, per session.

| Level | Name | Indicative frequency (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Improbable | < 1 in 10,000 sessions |
| P2 | Remote | 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000 |
| P3 | Occasional | 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000 |
| P4 | Probable | 1 in 10 to 1 in 100 |
| P5 | Frequent | > 1 in 10 |

**Acceptability matrix (proposed):** A = acceptable, R = reduce as far as possible and justify by benefit-risk, U = unacceptable.

| | P1 | P2 | P3 | P4 | P5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **S5** | R | U | U | U | U |
| **S4** | R | R | U | U | U |
| **S3** | A | R | R | U | U |
| **S2** | A | A | R | R | R |
| **S1** | A | A | A | A | R |

Probabilities below are engineering estimates. Where real-video measurements exist ([VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md)), they are cited. All other values are judgement, not measurement.

## 4. Residual risk summary

| Residual class (proposed matrix) | Hazards |
|---|---|
| Unacceptable unless further controls or labelling restrict use | H-01 (missed events, S3/P4), H-04 (camera motion, S3/P4), H-13 (data at rest, S4/P3) |
| Reduce as far as possible; justify by benefit-risk | H-02, H-03, H-05, H-06, H-08, H-11, H-12, H-14, H-16, H-17, H-18 |
| Acceptable as proposed | H-07 (S2/P2), H-09, H-10, H-15, H-19 |

**Overall residual risk (engineering view):** for the **current labelled use** (research/demo on synthetic or staged consenting-adult footage, single local user), the residual risks are considered manageable, mainly because no clinical decision should depend on the output. For **any clinical use**, the overall residual risk cannot be judged acceptable today. Detection performance is modest and supported only by non-clinical engineering samples (19 handheld Charades stand-up windows and 40 action clips, plus 195 stand-ups in 506 fixed-camera lab videos of adults from UTKinect-Action3D and PKU-MMD; none from a clinical setting). Usability has not been evaluated. Data-at-rest protection depends entirely on deployment controls the app cannot enforce. A benefit-risk analysis needs clinical evidence that does not yet exist ([clinical_validation_plan.md](clinical_validation_plan.md)).

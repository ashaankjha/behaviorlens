## What this is about

We are developing computer software that watches a video of a person and **suggests** moments when certain simple movements happen: raising a hand above the shoulder, standing up from a chair, and sitting down. A person always checks the suggestions. The software does not diagnose anything, and it does not judge emotions, intentions or behaviour.

To test whether the software works, we need videos of adults acting out these movements in a room set up like a table-work area. **You would be one of the people in those videos.** This is research and development only. It is not a medical or clinical study, and it is not therapy.

## What you would do

- Sit at a table and follow simple spoken instructions for about [60–75] minutes, with breaks: stand up, sit down, raise a hand, stretch, reach for things, and similar.
- Be filmed by one camera on a tripod. Your **face and whole body will be visible**.
- In some takes, another adult volunteer may walk past or sit nearby.

You can skip any movement, take a break, or stop at any time, without giving a reason.

## Who can take part

Adults aged 18 or over who can comfortably stand up from and sit down on a chair several times. **Please don't tell us about any health conditions.** We don't need that information, and we won't record it.

## What happens to the videos

- **Stored locally only.** Videos are kept on [an encrypted computer / encrypted drive] controlled by [researcher/organisation]. They are **not uploaded** to any cloud service, website or video platform, and they are **not sent to any AI service or chatbot**. The software processes them on that local computer.
- **Prepared for analysis.** A copy of each video is made with the **sound removed** and hidden file information (like the phone's location) stripped. The copy is given a random code instead of a name. The original recording [may contain sound and] is kept only until [the copy has been checked / date], and then deleted.
- **Who can see them:** only [named roles: the researcher, up to N trained annotators] for this project. Annotators watch the videos to mark when movements happen.
- **What gets shared:** only **numbers** about how well the software performs, such as "it found 40 of 50 stand-ups", in reports or publications. **No video, still images, names or descriptions that identify you** will be shared or published.
- **Your name** is kept only on this form and on a code list, both stored separately from the videos. The videos are labelled with a code such as "P07".
